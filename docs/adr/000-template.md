# ADR-NNN — Short title in the imperative

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN

## Date

YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces at play: what problem appeared, what constraints apply, what was
already true when the decision had to be made. Enough that a reader who was not
there can judge whether the decision still holds. State unverified assumptions
explicitly.

## Considered options

### Option A

Rejected, deferred or accepted, and why in one or two sentences.

### Option B

Same.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it commits the project to.
Include the cost of reversing it. A consequence that is only positive is
usually an incomplete analysis.
